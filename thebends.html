The Bends - Radiohead

The most “sonically palatable” Radiohead album. I thoroughly enjoyed this one. Not really the sound that they are known for, but a testament to their ability as musicians to make things that sound good.

I was watching How I Met Your Mother and in the episode where Ted gets hit by a car Nice Dream was playing and I thought that was cool.

I used to not be a fan of Fake Plastic Trees because I always felt it was pretty surface level and nothing special, as well as being a bit too similar to Elton John’s Rocket Man.
My mind was changed when my soon to be girlfriend told me she loved it.

I got really sick my senior year of high school all of a sudden and collapsed on my bed at 3 PM. I had both earbuds in and I had Nice Dream and Black Star playing on repeat and it was a pretty transformative experience to hear that while being maybe 1/3 conscious.



The ranking of the songs is as follows:
Sulk > Bones > Planet Telex > Just > My Iron Lung > The Bends > High and Dry > Fake Plastic Trees > (Nice Dream) > Bulletproof… I Wish I Was > Black Star > Street Spirit (Fade Out)

About as close to a perfect album as you can get, an encapsulation of a genre and an era, for me 10/10.
