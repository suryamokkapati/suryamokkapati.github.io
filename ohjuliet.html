"Indie rock band from Lafayette, LA comprised of vocalist Cullen Bernard, drummer Josh Hefner, guitarists Thomas Onebane and Patrick Trumps, and bassist Joshua Wells. Their first and only official release was the EP 'A Southern Boy's Guide To Raising The Dead', which was independently released on May 1st, 2007 to moderate local success. Following the band's dissolution three of the original five members would go on to be members of Royal Teeth, though only one remains to this day." - cynicalseraph, Genius
