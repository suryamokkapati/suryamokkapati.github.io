Waltz for Debby - Bill Evans Trio

Very dreamy, kind of pastel-like. The piano is very polite, but at times leaves the fragility and becomes wistful and fuller.

This ismy favourite jazz piano album of all time, it is perfect from all angles. Waltz for Debby is perfect, My Romance is perfect.

An underrated track is Davis' Milestones. The whole trio is completely locked in for the entire track.

10/10 Album
