Those Once Loyal - Bolt Thrower

10/10 for its genre. I used to listen to a lot of Bolt Thrower when I was a freshman in high school. I would put it at Bolt Thrower's best album, though there an argument for IVth Crusade.
At First Light is such an insane opening to an album, and the title track is poetic. I read that Karl Willets visited the Flanders Fields when creating this album, and the Great War influence is heavy.
What is interesting is how apparent it is that they are speaking of the horrors of war, not glorifying it.
